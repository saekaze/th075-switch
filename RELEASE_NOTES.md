## Touhou 7.5 — 1.11-r1 (first release)

A native Nintendo Switch port of Touhou 7.5: Immaterial and Missing Power ver1.11, built on SteinsGateON's TH075 C++ reimplementation (published by Goan114) and compiled natively for ARM64.

* 🥊 **The full game:** Story, Arcade, Versus (player or CPU), Training, Replays, Music Room, results and the card list.
* 🎮 **Local 2-player:** Joy-Cons are player 1, a Pro Controller (or any other controller) is player 2.
* 🔊 BGM streamed straight from `th075bgm.dat` with the original loop points.
* 🈂️ Japanese text from `msgothic.ttc` (or the Switch's font), drawn as needed — no long first launch.
* 📁 Recommended folder: `sd:/switch/touhou/touhou075/`, next to the other ports.

Put `touhou075.nro`, `th075.dat`, `th075b.dat`, `th075bgm.dat` and `msgothic.ttc` (ver1.11, Japanese) in the folder and launch from hbmenu.

Controls: B = A (weak, confirm), A = B (strong, cancel), Y or L/ZL = C (spell card), X or R/ZR = D (dash), + = pause, − = snapshot.
